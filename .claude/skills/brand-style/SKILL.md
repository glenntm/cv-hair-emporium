# Brand style

- Feel: bold and high-contrast, like a salon wordmark glowing on a black bar over full-bleed hair photography. Direct, not soft or spa-like.
- Colors: violet #9400FF (the only accent — buttons, links on hover, small labels), near-black #1F1F1F (nav and footer), ink #1A1A1A (headings), white #FFFFFF / #FAFBFC (text on dark and page background). A lilac band #9694FF is used for the stats strip only. Gold #FFBA43 and blue #AED2FF are defined in CSS and are not part of the live look.
- Fonts: Archivo for everything. Headings at weight 900. Small labels above headings are uppercase, weight 700, letter-spacing 0.1em, in violet. Body is 16px, weight 400, black, line-height 1.5, and stays under about 40rem wide.
- Sections get 60–100px of vertical padding and 16px on the sides. Content sits in a column up to 80rem. Leave room between a heading, its note, and the block under it.
- Buttons are violet, white, weight 700, with 4px corners. On hover they fill to #1A1A1A from the left. Icon controls are circles. Do not round cards to 12px or add soft drop shadows; the site is mostly flat, and the glow belongs on the logo.
- Build every section for a phone width first. The nav collapses under 1024px. The hero uses a still image under 766px and the carousel above that.
